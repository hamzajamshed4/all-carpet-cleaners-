Privacy Policy

This privacy notice is for All Carpet Cleaners (ALL CARPET CLEANERS LTD), a small carpet cleaning business based in Bristol.

What we collect

This website does not use online contact forms. If you call us, we may take down details you give us over the phone — such as your name, address, and phone number — so we can provide a quote or arrange a visit.

How we use your information

We use contact details only to respond to enquiries and to carry out cleaning work you have asked us to do. We do not sell your information to anyone else.

Cookies and analytics

This site itself does not set tracking cookies. If Google Analytics (or a similar tool) is added later, that tool may collect anonymous usage data such as page views. We will update this page if that changes.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask us what personal information we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it where appropriate. To do that, call us on 07853 537215.
